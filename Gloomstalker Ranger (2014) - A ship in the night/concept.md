# Background

Grenn Jassan is a half-elf ranger from Orlumbor.

He grew up in the simple community of herders and shipwrights. 

His father (Mehmen, human) was a cleric of Illmater and acted as a local healer, his younger brother (half-elf, Brennen)  is studying to follow the same path.

Grenn, a believer but not quite as devout, elected to train as part of the small towne guard. Grenn was trained as a fighter, and took station at the port where he was introduced to the criminal underbelly.

Contraband, people of interest, and anything else you could imagine. For the right price, the guards of Orlumbor would falsify your paperwork, and even accompany you through to your destination.

Grenn knew this was wrong, but the weight of his coin purse after the first job was more than enough to soothe his aching conscience.

Over time Grenn became quite well connected as a smuggler, his territory expanded and over time he's become an expert in smuggling all throughout the sword coast.

Several weeks prior, Grenn left on one such job with a human pirate, and when they returned to port he was met with the news of an attack.

The cult of the dragon attacked the village and naturally, his father tried to take as many injured citizens in as possible.

Eventually the cult breached the home, and Mehmen was killed. Brennen fought back as well, but his body was never recovered. Some villagers believe the cult dragged him back to their ship and sailed him to the mainland for some reason.

Grenn reaches through his network of thieves and smugglers and eventually gets a lead on a large cell of the cult moving northbound. We find Grenn stumbling into the lizard folk uprising along the way.
