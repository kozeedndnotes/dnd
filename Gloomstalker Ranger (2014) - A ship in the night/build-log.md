# Level 1

## Race
- Grenn is a half-elf
- Here we take a +2 to dex, and +1 to wis and int to round out our ability scores
- We also take two proficiencies, I took sleight of hand and pursuasion, I didn't pick these based on logical source, just listed what I wanted (in total) and grabbed them.
- We get darkvision of 60 feet, which we can expand with our subclass choice
- We also get undercommon as a third language.

## Class
- Grenn started out training with the city guard, so it makes sense that he'd start as a Fighter 1.
    - We're taking perception and survival for our skills
    - As Grenn isn't particularly strong, he's been trained primarily with a longbow and light weapons.

## Background
- Grenn is a criminal - he's become a talented smuggler that just seems to 'vanish' into the shadows at a moment's notice.
- This is also a pretty good tie-in for how Grenn finds out about the cult of the dragon's movement up north.
- Grants us stealth and deception proficiency

## Starting Equipment
- Leather armor (at 20 dex this is better than scale mail)
- Longbow
- Heavy Crossbow
- Scimitar
- Light Crossbow
- Explorer's pack
- Crowbar, dark clothes, pouch (background)

## Free Feat
- Since all characters are granted a free feat, I'm taking Elven Accuracy.
- I mostly just want a dex-based half feat and this seems the most straightforward.

## Magic Items
- Along Grenn's journey he's been paid in things other than gold. 
- +1 Longbow (I also considered a weapon of warning, but I know that can take some of the fun out of travel if we can never be surprised..)
- Boots of Elvenkind
